## Third-party source material

Third-party source material, titles, extracts, standards and official publications remain subject to their owners' terms. They are excluded from the project's AGPL, CC BY and CC0 grants unless an item states otherwise. Metadata-only classification permits metadata and a source link, not storage or republication of the source text.

Live-v2 validation requires the producer contract's exact observation-specific rights mode, attribution and licence URL. For the frozen asset, that means `metadata-only`, the exact attribution template rendered with the observation's checked date and `https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`. The canonical record carries those fields forward. They are asset-specific metadata, not a blanket licence for the source, later observations or legislation text.

A content hash identifies which bytes supported an observation, GitHub provenance identifies their production history, and attribution identifies the credited source. None of them grants a licence to copy, store or republish the bytes. This integration stores or publishes no legislation text and no source extract, quotation, source HTML, PDF or EPUB. Extract-bearing work requires a separately approved rights and quotation policy.
